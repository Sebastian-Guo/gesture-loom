QUICK START

Bring both hands into view.

  1. Choose “Start immersive drawing” for the full-window experience, then click “Enable camera” and allow camera access. The tracking model needs a moment to load on first use.
  2. Face your palms toward the camera, about half an arm to an arm away. Keep all ten fingers visible in good lighting.
  3. Extend to draw, curl to thin, and fold to lift. Press Space to pause while repositioning your hands.
  4. Ribbons connect adjacent fingers on each hand. Web mode connects matching fingertips across two visible hands.
  5. Immersive mode combines your artwork with translucent black hand silhouettes that fade at the wrists. Shadows follow tracked landmarks, including curled fingers, and stay live when drawing is paused. Press Escape to return to the split view. Export saves the artwork without shadows.
  6. Adjust brush size, smoothing, and opacity. Export saves the artwork only. Refreshing or closing this page discards unsaved artwork.
  7. Turn on “Fade after 3s” to keep each mark for 3 seconds, then fade it out over 2 seconds. Existing artwork starts its timer when enabled. Turn it off to preserve the remaining marks. Fading continues while drawing is paused; restored artwork starts a new timer.

The demo uses simulated hands, not your camera. Tracking runs locally in your browser; video is never uploaded. Overlaps, occlusion, and fast movement can interrupt strokes. Twisting a finger around its own axis is not tracked.

If camera access is blocked in an embedded window, open this site in a separate browser tab and check its camera permission.
